SUBJECT: Re: How do I change my website logo?
Hi Maria,

To replace the default logo, log in to the Admin Area and go to **Settings → General Settings**. Open the **Brand Images** tab, scroll to **Website Logo**, click **Replace Image** (or the red X to remove the current logo first), upload your new logo file, and then save the changes. Your new logo will appear on the site.

If you prefer using the Logo Designer, go to **Settings → Design Settings**, open the **Logo Designer** section, clear any existing text, design or upload your new logo, and save.

Let us know if you need any further help.
