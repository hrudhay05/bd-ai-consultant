SUBJECT: Re: How do I change my website logo?
Hi Maria,

To replace the default logo, follow these steps:
1. Log in to the Admin Area.
2. Go to Settings → General Settings.
3. Open the Brand Images tab.
4. In the Website Logo section, click the red X to remove the current logo (optional), then click Replace Image and upload your new logo file.
5. Save the changes.

If you prefer using the Logo Designer, go to Settings → Design Settings, clear any existing text in the Logo Designer and Logo Slogan fields, design or upload your new logo, and click Save Changes.

Let us know if you need any further assistance.
